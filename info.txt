abstract
AI-powered full-stack web application for analyzing resumes.
 Extracts relevant skills and information from uploaded resumes.
 Generates an overall resume/ATS score with actionable feedback.
 Provides actionable suggestions to improve resume quality.
Recommends suitable job roles and current job openings based on resume–job matching.
 Uses Ollama for intelligent resume analysis.


objective
To develop an AI-based system for automated resume analysis.
 To identify and extract important skills and information from resumes.
 To evaluate the quality and effectiveness of resume content.
 To generate a resume score based on the analysis.
To identify skill gaps and recommend suitable career roles.
To match resumes with relevant job descriptions and job openings.
 To provide meaningful suggestions for improving resumes.
 To assist users in discovering relevant job opportunities.

SCOPE of the Problem Statement
Automates the initial analysis of resumes using  AI.
 Helps students and job seekers identify weaknesses in their resumes.
 Provides personalized feedback instead of relying only on manual review.
 Can assist users in improving resume content before applying for jobs.
 Integrates job suggestions based on the user's profile and resume.
Skill Gap Analysis – identifies missing/high-priority skills for target roles.
 Can be extended with additional AI models, job platforms, and advanced resume metrics.
Resume Version Tracking – compares versions and shows improvement over time.
